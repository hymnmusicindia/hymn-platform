# Promotion artwork

Platform marks are original raster assets, not redrawn SVG logos. They identify the destination or service and do not imply platform or artist endorsement.

- `youtube-music-original.png`: original YouTube Music logo by YouTube, PNG rendition from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:YouTube_Music_logo.svg. Source: https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/04/YouTube_Music_logo.svg/960px-YouTube_Music_logo.svg.png. Used unmodified, on a white background to preserve the black wordmark.
- `youtube-overview.webp`: original example analytics screenshot from https://artists.youtube/resources/analytics-for-artists/. Source: https://www.gstatic.com/marketing-cms/assets/images/8f/fb/a478b82b4b3ba116ccc7f93ae792/overview-tab.webp. This is YouTube's example data, not HYMN or Shreya Ghoshal's analytics.
- `youtube-analytics.webp`: original YouTube educational resource artwork. Source: https://www.gstatic.com/marketing-cms/assets/images/1d/09/99e4dfaf4c3e930c3bf8a8300d2d/analytics-artists.webp.
- `youtube-oac-guide.webp`: original YouTube educational resource artwork. Source: https://www.gstatic.com/marketing-cms/assets/images/4f/1d/8d2f48ef47a5990c4c491ac1e43f/432a832ebfadbbace874ca5fd0fe4e1c4fd2a5c2.webp. Both resource artworks link to YouTube for Artists.
- `shreya-ghoshal.jpg`: Bollywood Hungama / Wikimedia Commons, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Shreya.Ghoshal.jpg, CC BY 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/. The original asset remains unmodified; CSS crops its display. Visible attribution is on the OAC page; the Promotion card links there. The channel composition is illustrative.
- Spotify wordmark: existing `public/assets/store-logos/wordmark-spotify.png`, preserved without redraw or recolouring. Links lead to Spotify's own artist resources.
- Role photographs: existing `public/assets/onboarding/` assets; sources and Unsplash licensing are recorded in that directory's `SOURCES.md`.
- Playlist artwork: existing `public/assets/playlist-images/previews/` catalogue assets.

Typography: original Inter variable font from https://github.com/rsms/inter, locally served as `/fonts/inter-variable.woff2`. SIL Open Font License is preserved at `/fonts/inter-LICENSE.txt`.

- `youtube-music-transparent.png`: original transparent dark-background Music mark from YouTube for Artists, https://www.gstatic.com/marketing-cms/assets/images/fa/46/39b7e6e54863a5a9c68c8671db2b/logo.webp. Converted to PNG as requested, preserving colour and alpha.

The OAC analytics preview now uses a code-rendered sample dashboard, clearly labelled sample data. It contains no provider tutorial annotations and represents no artist or customer account. The previous tutorial screenshots remain source assets only.

- `community-concert.jpg`: Jonathan Ikemura, https://unsplash.com/photos/crowd-with-hands-up-at-a-bright-concert-stage-z0R4XB25ozI, Unsplash License. Local 1600px rendition; concert audience used for community artwork.
- `record-rights.jpg`: Vincent Chan, https://unsplash.com/photos/vinyl-record-on-turntable--ja1xVOENMo, Unsplash License. Local 1400px rendition; recorded music / release preparation artwork.

Navigation motion references: https://m3.material.io/styles/motion. Shared chrome remains in the root layout; menu motion is brief and disabled for reduced motion.
