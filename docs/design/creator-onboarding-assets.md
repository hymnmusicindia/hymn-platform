# Creator onboarding illustrations

Current direction: premium editorial doodles with charcoal ink outlines, porcelain and slate-grey fills, sparse steel-blue accents, transparent PNG backgrounds. Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. These are fictional illustrative characters, not customer testimonials. The floating workspace detail cards are rendered in code.

## producer

Final asset: public/assets/creators/producer-doodle-v1.png

Prompt:
Use case: illustration-story. Create one isolated premium editorial doodle illustration for HYMN music creator onboarding: a friendly young adult music producer with wavy hair, headphones around their neck, using a silver laptop and compact MIDI keyboard. Cohesive sophisticated hand-drawn cartoon aesthetic: confident slightly irregular charcoal ink outlines, simple expressive face with dot eyes, gently exaggerated proportions, flat porcelain-white and cool slate-grey fills, very sparse muted steel-blue accents, subtle fine pencil hatching only on clothing. Friendly, art-directed independent music magazine illustration, warm personality, elegant sparse shapes, mature and premium rather than childish. Waist-up seated composition with person and devices fully readable, compact balanced silhouette, no surrounding room or desk, generous transparent margin around silhouette. Genuinely transparent PNG alpha background. NO photographic rendering, no realistic skin texture, no 3D, no glossy shading, no gradients, no green, no text, no logos, no watermark, no background rectangle or baked checkerboard. The laptop and equipment should use simple clear shapes and a few hand-drawn details. All linework must remain legible on graphite backgrounds; use porcelain and pale grey fills inside the character silhouette. Landscape 3:2 composition.
