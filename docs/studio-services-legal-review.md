# Studio Services legal and privacy review

Status: **Human legal review required before production launch.**

The draft product language in `lib/policy-content.ts` covers project scope, source-material responsibility, customer authority, engineer obligations, revision definitions, paid revisions, scope amendments, turnaround estimates, delivery, approval and auto-completion, cancellations, refunds, disputes, prohibited conduct, confidentiality, platform role, payment, commission, file retention, and private-file processing.

Counsel must review the wording against the jurisdictions where HYMN and its users operate, the actual Razorpay settlement arrangement, tax treatment, consumer cancellation rules, limitation-of-liability requirements, copyright/work-for-hire expectations, data retention schedules, dispute handling, and the final engineer agreement. Product copy must not describe HYMN as a regulated escrow provider unless counsel and the payment architecture support that claim.

Private Studio audio and messages are restricted to project participants and authorized operations staff. They must not be used for unrelated model training, promotion, or other secondary purposes. Deletion handling must preserve only records needed for transaction history, licences, fraud controls, disputes, accounting, or legal obligations.
