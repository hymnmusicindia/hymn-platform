# Daily call planning

Team OS's existing five-minute cron now plans call work after customer follow-ups, artist journeys and promises are reconciled. It uses real source tasks from callbacks, enquiries, onboarding, distribution help/corrections, beat licensing, studio/promotion interests and artist promises. It does not fabricate leads, place calls or send customer outreach automatically.

Each recognized active employee caller receives one versioned updated-duty task, plus one planning card per India workday with available call capacity. Owners retain their existing separate opt-in personal plan. Existing responsibilities, source records, research attribution and financial ownership remain intact; role permissions are not expanded. Unavailable employees or days with no working capacity do not receive a daily planning card. The permanent duty update can be reviewed when they return.

The duty card explains contact preparation, personalized guides, new module context, live activity reports, promises, individually reviewed email templates, follow-up scheduling and authorized escalation. It appears in the existing Work-station in both the web workspace and Android app. Source call tasks remain the records of real calls; the daily planning card does not count as customer contact evidence.

Calls reserve 30 minutes, respecting the current shift, weekly overrides, leave, conflicts, customer contact preferences/timezone and requested future dates. Available shift minutes cap the day's volume. A positive configured call target is an additional ceiling; hybrid researchers without a separate positive call target default to at most four call reservations per day. Existing calls and standalone customer appointments consume capacity. Shared availability-slot allocation applies these limits to upstream module-generated calls too.

Urgent explicit help/callbacks and overdue work rank above inferred engagement. Eligible reps are ranked by weighted existing workload, with optional `crm.callServices` specialization. Started or paused activities, pinned assignments, owners' calls, confirmed customer appointments and future scheduled work are preserved. Opt-outs, suppressed/unverified/ineligible prospects, internal contacts, inactive accounts and invalid phone numbers are held. The same customer or normalized phone is not automatically scheduled twice in a day by this planner.

Workspace locking serializes assignments and availability changes. Stable per-day/version keys deduplicate plans, duties and notices. Existing calendar links are updated rather than duplicated. A bounded queue cursor prevents a page of invalid old tasks from permanently blocking later work. Each run has an 18-second planning budget and schedules at most twelve calls; following cron runs continue remaining work. Source tasks without capacity stay pending. Internal follow-up reservations notify reps through existing infrastructure and do not claim to be confirmed customer appointments.

`scripts/apply-daily-call-schedule.ts --workspace=<verified-id>` previews today's counts. `--apply` runs bounded planning immediately with the same workspace lock and owner revalidation used by cron. The operator supplies credentials through the environment; none are stored in this script.

Verification: isolated PostgreSQL daily planning tests cover capacity, updated module priority, balancing, active/pinned/confirmed work, unavailable reps, invalid phones, requested future dates, repeat-run idempotency, overlapping slots, owner exemption, authorization and no customer appointment mail. The existing call-plan and workstation regressions cover shifts, call retries, history and concurrency. No live customer contacts are called or emailed by these tests.
